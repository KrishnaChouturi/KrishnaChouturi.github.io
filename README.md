# Krishna Chouturi — Portfolio

View the portfolio at **https://krishnachouturi.github.io/**. Photos and the Smart Garden video open directly on the website; no download is needed.

Published through GitHub Pages from the main branch.

## Preview on your computer

1. Select **Code → Download ZIP** on this repository.
2. Extract the ZIP (on Windows, right-click → **Extract All**).
3. Open `index.html` in your browser. Keep the `media` folder next to it so the photos and video load.

## Edit

All page text and styles are in `index.html`; no installation or build step is needed. Photos, screenshots, and the Smart Garden video are in `media/`.

The Weather Station uses photos only. Smart Garden includes the supplied 30-second demonstration. Side projects include World Cup Simulator, StudyVault, and The Legend of Two Thrones.

Optional engineering and side-project templates remain hidden in the HTML. Fill in a template and remove its `hidden` attribute when ready. The editing guide near the top of `index.html` explains how to add media.
